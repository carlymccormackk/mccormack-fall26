Hannah Wells

Portrayed by Ella Bright

Born: 2004

Gender: Female

Occupation: Student at Briar University & Waitress at Malone’s

Hannah Wells, the main character in the Prime Video series Off Campus, is portrayed by Ella Bright. Hannah is a straight-A student majoring in music composition. She is crushing hard on Justin Khol, leading to her striking a deal with Garrett Graham to have a fake relationship. He’ll pose as her boyfriend to make Justin jealous, and she’ll tutor Garrett to pass their philosophy class. Hannah is from Ransom, Indiana, and came to Briar University to pursue her dreams in music. She wasn’t the most outgoing and social girl in high school but attended a party one night where she was roofied and raped by the mayor's son, Aaron Delaney. She reported the assault, and the case went to trial, but she lost the case to the status and support Delaney had for him. She began going to therapy to help her through her trauma, which was challenging for her. She lives on campus with her best friend, Allie Hayes.

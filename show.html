Season 1 of the Prime Video original series, Off-Campus, created by Louisa Levy, is based on the best-selling book series Off Campus by Elle Kennedy. The show takes place on the fictional campus of Briar University. The show follows four close hockey players; Garrett Graham, John Logan, Dean Di Laurentis, and John Tucker. Throughout the first season of the show, we see these characters as they begin to navigate their adulthood and relationships. 

This first season ultimately focuses on Hannah Wells and Garrett Graham, and the story of their relationship. The season follows their fake-dating agreement that turns into a deep romance. This agreement or “the deal” revolved around Hannah’s crush on Justin Khol, a musician, and Garrett’s need for tutoring to pass his philosophy class. So, their deal was that Hannah would tutor Garrett, and Garrett would be Hannah’s fake boyfriend to make Justin jealous. Both Hannah and Garrett are dealing with heavy baggage and past trauma in their lives. Garrett was dealing with emotional and physical abuse from his father, Phil Graham. Hannah is a survivor of sexual assault, who is suffering with intimacy. As they both shared these past traumas with one another, they began to develop genuine feelings. 

This season also begins to follow the relationship between Allie Hayes and Dean Di Laurentis. Allie is Hannah’s best friend, and Dean is a close friend and teammate of Garrett Graham. Allie and Dean’s relationship is more so a secret in comparison to Hannah and Garretts. Their attraction with each other begins during parties and social events, leading to their inevitable hookup. At this time, Allie is still dealing with her past relationship with Sean. 

Overall, Season 1 of Off Campus focuses on friendship, trauma, personal growth, sexuality, and the development of their adult lives. This first season consists of 8 episodes, streaming on Prime Video. The series was renewed for season 2, which will shift focus to Allie and Deans' relationship as the main focus.

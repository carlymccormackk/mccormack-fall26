The best-selling book series Off-Campus was written by Elle Kennedy. The first book of the series is The Deal, published in 2015. This first book of the series follows the relationship between Hannah and Garrett and is the main source for season 1 of the show, Off Campus. The Deal follows Hannah Wells, a music student at Briar University and Garrett Graham, one of the star hockey players at the university. Hannah has a crush on Justin, a football player, which is ultimately how Hannah and Garretts deal came to be. Their deal was for Garrett, who was struggling in their philosophy class, to be tutored by Hannah, while pretending to be her boyfriend to make Justin jealous. This arrangement between Hannah and Garrett is why the book is titled ‘The Deal.’

Throughout the book, Hannah and Garretts relationship shifts from fake to very real. They begin learning things about each other that most people don’t know about. They ultimately become very trusting of one another after sharing these traumatic past events. Garrett becomes someone whom Hannah is comfortable with in an emotional and physical sense. Hannah gives Garrett emotional support, allowing him to realize that he will not become like his father, who was emotionally and physically abusive towards him and his mother.

Hannah and Garrett fall in love, but are forced apart by Garretts father, Phil Graham, because he did not approve of their relationship and believed she was a distraction from hockey. Since Hannah doesn’t want Garrett to lose his future or his relationship with his family, she breaks up with him. This is a big difference from the show.

In Garretts devastation, he discovers the reason why Hannah broke up with him and confronted his father about it. This results in them getting back together at the end of the book. So, at the end of the book, Hannah and Garrett are happily back together, and the close friendships of Garrett, Logan, Dean, and Tucker are established, which continues throughout the book series.

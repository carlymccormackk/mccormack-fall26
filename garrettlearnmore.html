Garrett Graham

Portrayed by Belmont Cameli

Born: 2004

Gender: Male

Occupation: Student at Briar University & Star Forward for Briar Hawks Hockey Team

Garrett Graham, the main character in the Prime Video series Off Campus, is portrayed by Belmont Cameli. The character Garrett Graham is the confident son of NHL star player Phil Graham. He is the captain and star forward of Briar Universities Hockey team. His character originally comes off as an intimidating and cocky jock who only cares about hockey and didn’t have time for a relationship. As the season went on, it is evident that Garrett is actually a loyal and vulnerable character. When he was 11 years old, his mother passed away from cancer. His father was physically and verbally abusive towards him and his mother. We see his friendships with John Logan, Dean Di Laurentis, and John Tucker emotionally evolve throughout the season. They have deep and unexpected conversations about relationships. The love story between Garrett Graham and Hannah Wells is the mean focus of season 1. We see a fake relationship turn into a deep connection from past trauma.
